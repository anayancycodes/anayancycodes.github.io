This is the first post, so a quick note on what this place is.

I'm early in bioinformatics, and the resources that have helped me most weren't the polished tutorials where everything works on the first try. They were the ones where someone showed their *actual* working — the error message, the wrong turn, the moment it finally clicked. That's what I want to write.

## The plan

Three kinds of posts, roughly:

1. **Walkthroughs** — an analysis I'm learning, start to finish, with code you can run.
2. **Field notes** — short posts about one concept that confused me until it didn't.
3. **Workflow** — tools and setup, as I figure out what actually works.

## Why "in public"

A few honest reasons:

- Writing something down is the fastest way to find out whether I actually understand it.
- A searchable record of my own past confusion is worth more than any bookmark folder.
- If it helps one other person who's where I was six months ago, that's a good trade.

I'm not aiming for daily, polished, or comprehensive. The goal is *consistent and honest*. Some posts will be long walkthroughs; some will be three paragraphs. Both count.

If you're on a similar path, welcome. More soon.
